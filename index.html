About

What is ISTE?

The Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) student chapter connects curious engineers through hands-on learning. No prior experience needed — just bring curiosity.

🛠️ Workshops

Web dev, Python, Git, IoT, CAD, resume building and more — beginner-friendly and practical.

💡 Hackathons

Overnight builds, idea sprints and inter-college contests with mentorship and prizes.

🎙️ Tech Talks

Alumni, faculty and industry speakers on careers, higher studies and emerging tech.
