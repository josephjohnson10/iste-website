About Us

Who we are

The Indian Society for Technical Education is a national, professional, non-profit Society registered under the Indian Societies Registration Act of 1860.

What we do

To contribute to the development of skilled, innovative and industry-ready engineers and technical professionals who can meet the evolving needs of industries and organizations.

Our vision

To introduce several technological advancements of great importance to the student community.

Our mission

To enable students to explore the world of engineering and understand its scope.
